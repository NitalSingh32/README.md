TravelMate AI – Personalized Travel Itinerary Planner
About the Project
TravelMate AI is an AI-powered web application that helps users plan personalized trips based on their destination, budget, travel dates, interests, and travel preferences.
Features
Personalized day-by-day travel itineraries
Smart trip planning based on budget and interests
Attractions and dining recommendations
Estimated travel costs and budget breakdown
Interactive itinerary customization
Saved trips and trip management
Responsive and user-friendly dashboard
Technology Stack
React
TypeScript
Vite
Generative AI integration
How to Use
Enter your travel destination and dates.
Choose your budget and travel preferences.
Generate a personalized itinerary.
Review and customize your travel plan.
Project Objective
To simplify travel planning through AI-powered recommendations and help users organize convenient, personalized trips.
